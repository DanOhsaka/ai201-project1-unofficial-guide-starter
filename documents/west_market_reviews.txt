Source: RSU Off-Campus Housing & Dining Forum — thread "West Market worth the walk?"
URL: https://forum.rsustudents.org/t/west-market-reviews/8842

West Market is on the west side of campus near the engineering and science buildings. It operates as a hybrid: half convenience store, half hot food counter.

Hot food options: Build-your-own burrito bowl (most popular item), chicken tenders, pizza by the slice, and a daily special entree. The burrito bowl line is the bottleneck — students report 15-20 minute waits between 12:00 and 1:00 PM on Tuesdays and Thursdays when engineering labs let out.

Forum post by engineering_grad: "West Market is the only dining option within a 10-minute walk of the engineering quad. Food is decent, not great. The burrito bowls are Chipotle-adjacent — rice, beans, protein, toppings. Double chicken costs extra dining dollars. I eat here daily because North Hall is a 25-minute round trip."

Forum post by u/bio_major: "West Market has the best grab-and-go sushi on campus. Pre-packaged rolls restocked at 11 AM and 4 PM. They're gone by 12:15. The ramen bar opens at 5 PM for dinner — surprisingly good for campus food."

Forum post by u/mealplanmom: "Warning for parents: West Market prices add up fast without a meal plan. A burrito bowl with drink is $11.50. My kid burned through dining dollars in October. The convenience store side sells overpriced snacks — $4 for a bag of chips."

Convenience store inventory: Basic groceries (milk, bread, eggs), frozen meals, energy drinks, toiletries, and RSU merchandise. Prices are 20-30% higher than off-campus grocery stores.

Hours: Monday-Friday 7:30 AM - 11:00 PM, Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM.

Student consensus: Best for engineering/science students who need proximity. Food quality 3/5. Convenience 5/5. Wait times 2.5/5 at lunch peak.
