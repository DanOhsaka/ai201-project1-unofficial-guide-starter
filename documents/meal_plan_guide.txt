Riverside State University offers four meal plan tiers for 2024-2025. This guide compares them based on student experiences, not official marketing.

Unlimited Plan ($2,890/semester): Unlimited swipes into North Hall, East Commons, and the South Campus dining room. Best for freshmen living on campus who eat 2-3 meals daily at dining halls. Students report this is worth it if you eat on campus more than 14 meals per week. Includes $150 dining dollars for a la carte locations.

14-Meal Plan ($2,340/semester): 14 swipes per week at dining halls plus $300 dining dollars. Popular with juniors who cook some meals in apartment kitchens. Reset day is Monday at midnight. Unused swipes do NOT roll over — use them or lose them.

10-Meal Plan ($1,980/semester): 10 swipes per week plus $450 dining dollars. Best for students who skip breakfast and eat lunch+dinner on campus weekdays only. Many off-campus upperclassmen choose this.

5-Meal Plan ($1,200/semester): 5 swipes per week plus $600 dining dollars. Designed for commuters. Most students say this only works if you bring food from home most days.

Dining dollars: Loaded at the start of each semester. Can be used at South Quad Cafe, West Market, campus convenience stores, and food trucks. Dining dollars roll over fall to spring but expire at end of spring semester. Cannot be refunded for cash.

Money-saving tips from students:
- Buy the 14-meal plan, not unlimited, if you eat breakfast in your room (saves ~$550/semester).
- Spend leftover dining dollars at West Market convenience store in April — buy non-perishable snacks for summer.
- Guest swipes cost $12.50 each if a friend visits without a plan.
- Changing your meal plan is only allowed during the first two weeks of each semester.

Common complaint: RSU's official meal plan calculator on the bursar website underestimates how much dining dollars students need for coffee and snacks. Budget an extra $100-200 beyond what the calculator suggests.
