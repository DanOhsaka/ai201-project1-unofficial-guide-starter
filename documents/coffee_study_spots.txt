Source: RSU Study Spots & Coffee Guide (Notion page maintained by student ambassadors)
URL: https://notion.rsu-students.org/coffee-and-study-spots

Where to get coffee and study on the Riverside State University campus, with student ratings for both coffee quality and study environment.

South Quad Cafe: Best espresso on campus. Lattes, cappuccinos, cold brew, and seasonal specials (pumpkin spice in fall, lavender honey in spring). Prices: drip coffee $2.00, latte $4.25, cold brew $3.75. Study rating: 4.5/5 — outlets at every table, good lighting, allowed to stay for hours. Gets loud during class changes.

East Commons Coffee Bar: Drip coffee and basic espresso drinks included with dining hall meal swipe — no extra charge. Quality is average (2.5/5 for coffee) but unbeatable value if you're already eating there. Cannot enter just for coffee without a swipe or $12.50 guest entry.

BrewBike Coffee (food truck, Monday Library Plaza): Pour-over and cold brew. Cheapest quality coffee on campus ($2.50). No indoor seating — take it to the library. Student rating: 4/5 coffee, N/A for study (it's a cart).

North Dining Hall beverage station: Self-serve drip coffee, tea, and soft drinks included with meal swipe. Coffee quality is poor (1.5/5) — described as "brown water." Fine if you just need caffeine and you're already eating there.

Student Union — Riverfront Coffee: Third-party vendor, not run by campus dining. Higher quality than campus options (4/5). Prices are Starbucks-level ($5-6 for specialty drinks). Study rating: 3/5 — comfortable chairs but limited outlets and high foot traffic.

Library — 3rd floor silent study + coffee from vending: No real coffee shop on the 3rd floor, but vending machines sell canned cold brew ($3). Silent study policy makes it the best focus environment (5/5 study rating). Many students buy at South Quad and walk to the library.

Study spot rankings from student poll:
1. South Quad Cafe (coffee + study combo)
2. Library 3rd floor (focus, bring your own coffee)
3. East Commons patio (weather permitting, need meal swipe)
4. Engineering building lounge (mediocre coffee from vending, but quiet)

Coffee hack from u/espresso_nerd: "Buy a $15 French press, bring your own ground coffee, and use the hot water dispenser at North Hall beverage station. Baristas at South Quad respect the hustle."
