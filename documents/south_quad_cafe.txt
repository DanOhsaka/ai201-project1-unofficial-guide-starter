South Quad Cafe is a smaller, a la carte location in the South Quad academic building. Unlike the all-you-can-eat dining halls, you pay per item with dining dollars, credit card, or cash.

Menu highlights: Specialty coffee drinks (espresso, lattes, cold brew), fresh sandwiches on ciabatta, daily soup, pre-made salads, and a rotating hot entree. Popular items include the turkey avocado club ($8.50), caprese panini ($7.95), and the breakfast burrito ($6.25) served until 10:30 AM.

Student review compiled from Yelp (RSU South Quad Cafe, 47 reviews): "Best coffee on campus, period. The baristas actually know how to pull espresso. Lines are long during 10 AM class change but move fast — usually 5-8 minutes max."

Another Yelp review: "Sandwiches are fresh but overpriced without a meal plan. If you have dining dollars left at end of semester, this is where to spend them. The tomato bisque pairs well with the grilled cheese."

Reddit user u/studygrind: "South Quad Cafe is the move during finals week. Open until midnight during reading days. Quiet corner tables, outlets at every seat, and you can camp there with just a drip coffee for hours. No one kicks you out."

Hours: Monday-Friday 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM, Closed Sunday. Extended hours (until midnight) during final exam periods only.

Wait times: Morning coffee rush (8:45-9:15 AM) averages 10 minutes. Midday sandwich line (12:00-1:00 PM) averages 12-15 minutes. Off-peak is essentially walk-up.

Dietary notes: Gluten-free bread available on request. Oat milk and almond milk for coffee at no extra charge. One vegan sandwich option daily (usually hummus veggie wrap).
