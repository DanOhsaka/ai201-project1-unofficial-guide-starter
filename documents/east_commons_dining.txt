Source: RSU Student Government dining survey results (Spring 2024)
URL: https://sg.rsu.edu/dining-survey-2024

East Commons is the newest dining hall, opened in Fall 2023 in the East Campus residential village. It serves upperclassmen and graduate students primarily.

Survey findings for East Commons (412 student responses):

Food quality rating: 4.2/5 — highest of all campus dining locations. Students praised the international food station (rotates daily: Korean bibimbap Monday, Indian curry Tuesday, Thai noodles Wednesday, Mediterranean Thursday, Mexican Friday).

Wait times rating: 3.8/5 — better than North Hall because the facility was designed with four separate food islands to reduce bottlenecks. Average lunch wait reported as 8-12 minutes vs. 20+ at North Hall.

Ambiance rating: 4.5/5 — modern seating, floor-to-ceiling windows, outdoor patio open in warm weather. Described as "the only dining hall that doesn't feel like a cafeteria."

Meal plan usage: 67% of East Commons visitors have the unlimited plan. 33% pay per entry ($12.50 for non-plan guests).

Notable student comments from survey:
- "The sushi bar on weekends is a game changer. Fresh rolls made to order on Saturday and Sunday brunch."
- "Smaller than North Hall but every station is higher quality. The steak night on Thursdays is restaurant-level."
- "Too far from main campus if you don't live in East Village. I never go because it's a 20-minute bus ride."
- "Coffee bar with actual espresso drinks included in meal swipe — no extra charge unlike South Quad."

Brunch hours: Saturday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM. Weekday lunch: 11:00 AM - 2:30 PM. Dinner: 4:30 PM - 8:30 PM.

Accessibility: East Commons is the only dining hall with a dedicated allergen-free station (nut-free, gluten-free, dairy-free options prepared separately).
