Source: RSU Student Activities — Food Truck Schedule (scraped from campus events page, Fall 2024)
URL: https://activities.rsu.edu/food-trucks/schedule

Riverside State University hosts 4-6 food trucks on campus daily. Locations and schedules change each semester. Food trucks accept dining dollars, credit cards, and cash. They do NOT accept dining hall meal swipes.

Weekly recurring schedule (Fall 2024):

Monday — Library Plaza:
- Taco Loco (11 AM - 3 PM): Street tacos, burritos, quesadillas. Student favorite: carnitas burrito ($9). Vegetarian burrito with black beans and grilled peppers ($8).
- BrewBike Coffee (8 AM - 12 PM): Pedal-powered coffee cart. Pour-over and cold brew. Cheapest coffee on campus at $2.50.

Tuesday — Engineering Quad:
- Green Bowl (11 AM - 3 PM): 100% vegan menu. Buddha bowls, smoothie bowls, avocado toast. Club-rated best vegan option on campus (4.8/5). Popular: the "RSU Special" bowl with quinoa, chickpeas, kale, and lemon tahini ($10).
- Smash Burger Co. (11 AM - 7 PM): Smash burgers, fries, milkshakes. Long lines at lunch (15 min average).

Wednesday — Student Union:
- Seoul Kitchen (11 AM - 4 PM): Korean fried chicken, bibimbap bowls, kimchi fries. Spicy chicken sandwich is the top seller.
- Night Owl (9 PM - 1 AM, Thu-Sat only — see late_night_eating.txt)

Thursday — Library Plaza:
- Green Bowl (11 AM - 3 PM) — repeat from Tuesday.
- Slice Society (11 AM - 8 PM): New York style pizza by the slice. $4/slice, $18 whole pie. Garlic knots highly recommended.

Friday — Arts Quad:
- Curry Cart (11 AM - 3 PM): Indian street food. Butter chicken, samosas, naan wraps. Spice level customizable. Consistently rated 4.5/5.
- Dessert Truck "Sweet Wheels" (2 PM - 6 PM): Cookies, brownies, ice cream sandwiches.

Student tips:
- Food truck lines are shortest 11:00-11:30 AM and after 2:00 PM.
- Download the "RSU Eats" app for real-time truck locations — the printed schedule is often wrong when trucks cancel.
- Dining dollars work at all trucks but cannot be used for meal plan swipes.
- Green Bowl and Curry Cart sell out of popular items by 1:30 PM on busy days.
