URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/RSU/comments/late_night_eating_megathread

Riverside State University has limited late-night food options. Official dining hall dinner ends at 8:30 PM (East Commons) or 8:00 PM (North Hall). Here is what students actually do after hours.

West Market (open until 11 PM weekdays): The most reliable late-night option. Chicken tenders, pizza by the slice, ramen bar (after 5 PM), and the convenience store for instant noodles and snacks. Gets crowded 9:30-10:30 PM when students finish evening study sessions.

South Quad Cafe: Regular hours end at 8 PM but extends to midnight during final exam reading days only. Not available during normal weeks. Check the RSU academic calendar for reading day dates.

Night Owl food truck: Parks at the library plaza 9 PM - 1 AM Thursday through Saturday. Menu: loaded fries, chicken tenders basket, mozzarella sticks, and hot dogs. Accepts dining dollars and credit card. Average wait: 10 minutes. Reddit consensus: "greasy but hits the spot at midnight."

Campus convenience store in the Student Union: Open 24 hours. Sells frozen burritos you can microwave, instant ramen, cereal, milk, chips, and energy drinks. The microwave station is next to the checkout. Not real food but sustains many all-nighters.

Vending machines: Engineering building basement has the best vending selection (sandwiches, salads, hot food machine). Student Union machines are mostly chips and candy.

Off-campus options mentioned in thread: Jimmy's Diner (walkable, open until 2 AM), Pizza Palace (delivers to dorms until 11:30 PM), and the 24-hour Waffle House on Route 9.

Pro tip from u/nightowl_cs: "Keep $20 of dining dollars unspent for late-night West Market runs in November and April. You'll thank yourself during finals."

Pro tip from u/health_conscious: "The Night Owl truck has zero healthy options. If you want something lighter after 9 PM, your only bet is the convenience store salad cups — restocked at 8 PM but usually sold out by 10."
