Compiled tips from Riverside State University students on maximizing food value on campus.

Meal plan optimization:
- If you eat 2 meals/day on campus 5 days/week, the 14-meal plan beats unlimited by $550/semester. Use dining dollars for breakfast coffee at South Quad Cafe.
- Unused dining dollars expire in May — spend them in April on non-perishable items at West Market (protein bars, instant oatmeal, canned soup).
- The official meal plan calculator on the bursar site underestimates coffee/snack spending by $100-200. Plan accordingly.

Cheapest meals on campus (under $5 without meal plan):
- BrewBike Coffee pour-over: $2.50 (Monday, Library Plaza)
- West Market pizza slice: $3.50
- South Quad Cafe yogurt parfait: $4.50 (Saturday brunch only)
- North Hall with meal swipe: unlimited food per entry — best value per calorie if you have swipes

Free food opportunities:
- Club fairs in the Student Union (free pizza, snacks) — usually first week of each semester
- Department seminar events often have free food — check CS, Engineering, and Business department mailing lists
- RSU Plant-Based Students Club hosts free vegan potluck dinners monthly at the multicultural center
- "Donut Day" at North Hall — first Friday of each month, free donuts at breakfast (unadvertised, discovered by Reddit)

Money traps to avoid:
- West Market convenience store prices are 20-30% above grocery stores
- South Quad Cafe sandwiches without dining dollars ($7-9 each) add up fast
- Food truck meals average $9-11 per meal — more expensive than a dining hall swipe
- Vending machines in academic buildings charge $3-5 for items that cost $1 at a grocery store

Best value ranking (student vote): 1) Dining hall meal swipe, 2) Green Bowl truck (large portions), 3) Curry Cart, 4) West Market burrito bowl, 5) South Quad Cafe (lowest value without dining dollars).

Cooking in dorms: Microwaves allowed in all dorms. Mini fridges allowed. Hot plates banned in freshman dorms. Many students meal-prep rice and vegetables from West Market groceries on Sundays.
