Source: RSU Campus Life Blog (student contributor) — "Best Weekend Brunch on Campus"
URL: https://campuslife.rsu.edu/blog/weekend-brunch-guide

Weekend dining at Riverside State University is different from weekdays. Only two locations serve brunch, and hours are shorter.

East Commons (Saturday-Sunday 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM): The premier weekend brunch destination. Made-to-order omelet station, waffle bar with toppings (berries, whipped cream, chocolate chips), bacon and sausage, fresh fruit, pastries from the campus bakery, and the weekend-only sushi bar (11 AM - 1 PM). Bottomless drip coffee and juice included in meal swipe.

Student review: "East Commons brunch is the one meal that makes the unlimited plan worth it. Go at 9:30 AM to beat the 11 AM rush. The omelet line is 5 minutes early, 20 minutes at peak."

North Dining Hall (Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM): More casual brunch. Pancake station (self-serve batter on griddle), scrambled eggs, breakfast potatoes, cereal bar, and bagels with cream cheese. No omelet station. Less crowded than East Commons but lower quality according to student polls.

South Quad Cafe (Saturday only, 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM): A la carte brunch items — avocado toast ($5.95), breakfast burrito ($6.25), yogurt parfait ($4.50). Closed Sunday.

West Market (Saturday-Sunday 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM): No brunch service per se, but pre-made breakfast sandwiches and pastries available from 10 AM. Not a brunch destination.

Weekend dining tips:
- Both dining halls are significantly less crowded than weekday lunch. Average wait under 5 minutes before 11 AM.
- The 11 AM - 12:30 PM window is the new peak — students sleep in and all arrive at once.
- Guest swipes work on weekends — popular time for parents visiting campus.
- If you live in East Village, East Commons brunch is a 2-minute walk. From main campus dorms, take the Blue bus (runs every 15 minutes on weekends).

Food truck note: The Waffle Wagon parks at the Student Union on Sunday mornings 10 AM - 1 PM with Belgian waffles and coffee. Not part of meal plan — cash and card only.
